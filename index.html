Welcome to Los Santos' History

GTA Online launched on October 1, 2013. Since then it has grown from a simple multiplayer mode into a huge, constantly evolving world. Pick a year below to see how each update changed the game and how the community received it.

About GTA Online

GTA Online is the multiplayer part of Grand Theft Auto V, developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games. It opened two weeks after the release of the single-player game and takes place in the same fictional state of San Andreas.

Instead of playing as Michael, Franklin or Trevor, every player creates their own character and builds a criminal career: doing jobs for other characters, pulling off heists, running businesses across the state of Los Santos, aiming to be the criminal mastermind there is.

The game never stood still. Rockstar kept releasing free updates with new missions, vehicles, properties and game modes to, which is why this website is organised by year: each page covers the updates that arrived in that year and how the comunity recieved each one.
